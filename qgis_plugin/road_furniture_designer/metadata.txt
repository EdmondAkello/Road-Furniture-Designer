about=Design road furniture in QGIS next to the road alignment.

    Signs: click to place, or let the RDM 6.2 rules propose curve and grade warnings, chevrons and no-overtaking signs from the alignment's curves, grades and sight distances. Chainage, offset, side and facing come from the alignment and stay up to date when signs move. Speed limit zones are signed with limit signs, repeaters and end signs.

    Linear furniture: centre lines, edge lines and road studs from RDM 6.1 on the same no-overtaking basis as the signs; embankment barriers from the corridor sections and RDM 1.3 Chapter 12; humps, speed tables, bar markings and zebra crossings detailed to RDM 6.4 with their signs. Everything can also be drawn by hand.

    Junctions and widenings: junction islands, arrows and markings from the standard drawings, with the widenings they need and visibility splays checked against QGIS layers; a signalised crossroads; Junction Ahead warning signs; roundabouts (RDM 1.3 Chapter 10); private accesses; bus and truck bays, climbing and descending lanes, brake check areas and truck escape ramps (RDM 1.3 12.5, 12.7) proposed from the profile, with a widening table for Civil 3D and a check against the corridor when the LandXML comes back.

    Centreline-only LandXML: with only the alignment and its design and existing ground profiles, a typical section (RDM 1.3 standard sections or your own, by chainage range, superelevated from the LandXML) gives the road edges every tool uses, and the edges and widenings go back to Civil 3D as 3D feature lines.

    Outputs: a live inventory (Excel Tables and CSV) for the QTO; an A3 report with plan, long-section and strip sheets, schedules and checks of the furniture against the geometry; a georeferenced DXF that pastes into Civil 3D at true coordinates. Design criteria are open data files (Kenya RDM 2025 by default; AASHTO 2011 included).

    User Guide and Technical Manual (every tab, the report and its checks, and the methods, with figures): https://github.com/EdmondAkello/Road-Furniture-Designer/tree/main/docs/user-guide (PDF edition for each version). In QGIS: the Guide button on the dock, or Plugins > Road Furniture Designer > User Guide and Technical Manual.
